How a round goes

A round is always running. A new one starts every three minutes.

  1. 1

    Join the round

    The home screen shows the current board and how much time is left. One tap and you are in.

  2. 2

    Swipe across the letters

    Neighbouring tiles make a word, diagonals included. Longer words are worth more points.

  3. 3

    See what you missed

    You get the ranking for that round and every word that was hiding on the board.

Three leaderboards, three chances

Every round counts toward today, this week and this month. One good evening can put you on today's podium even when the month already belongs to someone else.

Built for one more round

Daily challenges and streaks
Three new challenges every day, and a streak that grows with each day you play.
English and Polish boards
The language of the words is separate from the language of the app, so you can practise either one.
No account needed
Open the app and play. Create an account later if you want to keep your scores.
